# AI economic survival and ramp materials

The AI now prices the complete remaining deep-extraction chain, including its prerequisite buildings/research and mine construction, in all four resources. Naval production, markets, optional construction, defenses and recruitment respect that commitment. Discovering another deposit no longer cancels it. Only known deposits with charted, reachable routes count toward the supply forecast. All costs, gathering rates, combat statistics and fog rules remain the normal player rules.

Resource voyages take priority when supplies run low. Workers keep their boarding assignments, use reachable deposits after landing, reserve a destination collection building and retry its construction. Surface and deep workers choose a collection point they can reach instead of the nearest building across water. Resource-route checks are cached for bounded periods to avoid repeatedly running the same searches.

Veteran and stronger opponents can add paid mines and short wall flanks around completed defensive towers, leaving a passage for their army. Master and stronger opponents can use a healthy fast unit as bait, withdraw toward a prepared mined position and counterattack a visible pursuer. This is a situational tactic, not control over the opposing player's units. Existing raids, coordinated attacks, counter selection and transport assaults remain active.

Ramps use the same material fields, vegetation, grain and palette as adjoining terrain. The pale sand/stone override is removed. Slope lighting, elevation, pathfinding and cliff-edge crowns are retained. Six-environment visual comparisons are in `ramp-material-review.png`. No campaign illustrations were changed.

Validation includes all twelve prerequisite budgets, persistent mine commitments after resource discoveries, island worker assignments, reachable drop-off buildings, paid defenses, bait withdrawal/counterattack, simulation and legacy regressions, strategic/tactical AI regressions, naval trade, all ten levels' fair rates and fog, and browser gameplay/offline launch. The endurance report records three allied Apocalypse opponents against a passive player on small islands, continuing after victory to inspect late extraction. It is an economic regression scenario, not competitive balance certification.

Refresh before playing. A new match is recommended for evaluating the improved AI; the update does not grant replacement resources to an old save whose AI has already spent everything. No strategy is guaranteed to survive loss of all workers, production and reachable resources.
