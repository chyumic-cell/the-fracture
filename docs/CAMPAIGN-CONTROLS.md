# Playing the story campaign

Open **Story Campaign** from the setup screen. Skirmish remains available below it.

- **New Campaign** starts at Mission 1. The confirmation screen replaces only campaign progress and its checkpoint.
- **Continue Campaign** restores the saved mission, a pending cinematic, or the next available briefing.
- **Mission Select** lists all 25 operations, grouped into four acts. Completed missions may be replayed; later missions remain locked.
- **Campaign Settings** chooses Easy, Standard, or Veteran for the next mission. Existing checkpoints retain their original difficulty.

The briefing shows the actual generated mission map, military objectives, starting civilization and field notes. Its preview marks bases and objective locations. **Start Mission** deploys the prepared map.

During play, the objective panel is at the upper left. Click an objective to center the camera on its location, or collapse the panel to uncover the battlefield. Gold diamonds mark active objective positions. The newest mission instruction appears below the objectives.

**F5** saves the campaign mission and **F9** restores it. Neither key touches the Skirmish save while a campaign is running. **Save checkpoint** updates the recovery checkpoint. Automatic checkpoints are taken at mission start and objective-stage changes unless disabled in Campaign Settings. Defeat offers checkpoint recovery or a full mission restart.

Victory is recorded before any cinematic starts. Continue opens the corresponding scene; skipping a scene still unlocks the next mission. Replaying an already completed mission does not duplicate completion records. Closing the browser during a cinematic preserves the pending scene for Continue Campaign.

Campaign Settings can export/import a separate campaign JSON file containing progress, the latest mission save and the recovery checkpoint. Export before moving the game to another browser or computer: browser local saves do not travel with the game folder.

## Story playback

The ten scenes contain five independent illustrations and passages each. Mission 1 precedes the first scene; the subsequent scene placements are after missions 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 19, 21, 24 and 25.

Playback provides narration play/pause, replay, seeking, volume, subtitles, a complete readable transcript and Continue/Skip. Escape skips the current scene. Cinematic Gallery replays unlocked scenes without changing progress.

When verified cue times are available, the current narration time controls the illustration and subtitle. A missing image or recording never blocks mission progression. If narration mapping or timings are unverified, the player explicitly presents manual passage controls rather than inventing synchronization.

**Edit audio timing** can select one of the bundled recordings and enter the exact start/end seconds of all five passages. Valid times must be ordered and non-overlapping, with each end after its start and inside the recording. Gaps may contain silence. **Use these verified timings** enables audio-clock synchronization for that scene on this computer. **Export timing manifest** saves these local mappings for review or incorporation into the game data.

The three children appear only in the story. Playable evacuation missions use unnamed adults; the player is never responsible for protecting Tarin, Kesh or Rook as gameplay entities.

A recording that ends before its scene is complete follows verified cues for the available narration, then explicitly switches to the remaining readable passages and illustrations. No missing narration is fabricated. Replay restarts the available recording; Continue/Skip still advances normally.
