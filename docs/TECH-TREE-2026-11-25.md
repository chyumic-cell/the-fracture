# Per-building tech tree — 25 November 2026

Requested after play-testing the six-doctrine tree: research was spread over different buildings,
some buildings could be built but trained nothing without research elsewhere, and some buildings had
an empty technology panel.

## Rules (all twelve civilizations, enforced by `tests/tech-tree.test.cjs`)

1. Research that unlocks units is done in the building that trains them. Each production building has
   up to two doctrines of its own; the second needs only the first.
2. No unit needs research from another building. The pace comes from the building chain (for
   example Humans: Barracks → Engineering Depot → Vehicle Factory → Airfield; the elite building also
   needs Heavy Armor Research) and from doctrine cost, which grows with the building's depth in that
   chain.
3. Every production building trains at least one unit straight away. Units that were already free stay
   free; if a building had none, its cheapest unit becomes free.
4. Every building with a technology panel has research. The lab keeps weapons, armor and economy
   upgrades. New upgrades:

| Building | Upgrade | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Headquarters | Field Logistics | +15% gathering for all workers |
| House | Expanded Quarters | each house supports 16 population instead of 12 |
| Defensive tower | Reinforced Emplacements | +20% range for that tower type |
| Market | Trade Contracts | +25% trade credits from sales (that player only) |
| Second lab-type building without units (Signal Spire, Pheromone Relay, Stone Circle, Treasure Vault, Sacrificial Altar) | building-specific survey | +2 sight for all units and buildings |
| Production building with no doctrine (single-unit elite buildings, Blood Research Institute) | "<building> Drills" | +20% training speed there |

How units are split: in each building, units are sorted by cost (giants last). The units that used
to be gated (heavy, support, tanks, siege, aircraft, elite, stealth, giants, air transports, and
anything that needed research) are split into the building's first and second doctrine, cheaper half
first. Ships keep their old rule: only the siege ship is gated, now by its own dock's doctrine.
Doctrines reuse each civilization's doctrine names where a building kind matches (infantry, vehicle
or beast, air, elite); other buildings use "<building> Doctrine" and "<building> Mastery". Duplicate
names within a civilization get the building name in brackets.

## Not measured

The road to the strongest units is shorter for some civilizations than under the six-doctrine tree,
because cross-building doctrine chains are gone (the building chain remains). No matchup timing or
balance was measured.
