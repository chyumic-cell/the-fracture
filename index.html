Field manual

Start with five workers and a headquarters. Build an army, scout the map, and destroy hostile headquarters and military production.

Select
Click or drag. Ctrl-click or Shift-click adds to selection. Double-click selects visible units of that type.
Order
Right-click ground to move, a deposit to gather, a friendly foundation to build, or an enemy to attack.
Facing
Right-drag from a destination toward the direction you want your formation to face.
Combat
A then click: attack-move. H: hold. S: stop. P then click: patrol. Q: unit ability.
Control groups
Ctrl + 1–9 saves a group. A number recalls it; Shift + number adds it to your current selection without changing the saved group. Press a number twice to center.
Camera
Arrow keys, screen edges, or middle-drag. Mouse wheel to zoom. Click the minimap to jump. Space centers on the current selection. Pause key or the top pause button pauses the match.
Economy
Ore funds basic units and buildings. Oil needs an extraction well beside its deposit. Aetherite and Rift Crystals are mined directly. Workers carry resources back to a headquarters or collection station.
Technology
Select a production building to train units or research. Humans, Reptilians and Machines have one physical extension per eligible building. Build extra copies for other branches.
Persistence
F5 saves locally. F9 loads. Escape cancels placement or opens the pause menu.
