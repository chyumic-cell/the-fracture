- Private scouting explores frontiers, avoids visible threats and remembers only observed enemy positions for a limited time. Targets are ranked by strategic value, exposed economy, known defenders and known terrain access. Hidden enemy/resource changes do not influence orders.
- Economic planning assigns workers to current demand, respects safe routes, budgets for concrete prerequisites/research/extensions, expands delivery infrastructure and selects counters from each faction's actual roster. It avoids redundant oil wells and foundations that would trap friendly units. Stalled builders can be replaced; a lone scout does not freeze the whole economy.
- Combat uses proportional defense, layered staging, matchup-aware commitment/retreat, fast flanks on known paths, target priorities, incoming-damage accounting, ranged spacing, wounded withdrawal, healer support and grouped reinforcements with a reserve. Repeated orders preserve committed attacks.
- Easy, Normal and Hard differ in decision pace, force commitment, workforce/army goals and tactical options, without resource/combat bonuses or omniscient navigation. Difficulty help text now describes these differences.
- Verification: 16 targeted AI behavior checks, 41 simulation checks, 11 legacy gameplay checks, 13 combat regression groups and the browser gameplay/save/load/offline suite passed. The 18 opening-match checks cover all twelve factions on all three difficulties. The frozen previous AI is retained in tests/fixtures for reproducible comparisons.
- The final side-swapped benchmark on two maps (Humans and Symbiotes) produced four wins for the new AI in four completed matches, with source hashes unchanged during the run. This is a bounded comparative result, not proof of complete cross-faction balance. Details and limitations: docs/AI-STRATEGY.md and docs/ai-benchmark-results.json.

## Maps, terrain, construction and controls - 8 November 2026

- Four map sizes: 96 x 96, 144 x 144, 192 x 192 and 500 x 500. Natural, Mountain Passes, Steppes and Labyrinth layouts are independent of the three world palettes. Layout generation protects connected routes from incidental resources and trees; labyrinth candidates are checked for winding routes between starts.
- Resource controls: Scarce/Standard/Rich/Abundant, random clusters/balanced territories/contested frontiers, and optional starting deposits. Settings and exact map state survive saves. Resources remain in homogeneous clusters.
- Code-authored grass, earth, rock, sand and shallow/deep water replace plain colors. Terrain/fog caches are bounded to 48 chunks each instead of full-world canvases; the 500-map browser test measured 99.8 MiB of combined chunk canvas pixels, excluding sprites, cached pixel tiles, GPU copies and other state. Texture tiles are cached as pixel arrays and each complete terrain region is rasterized into one pixel buffer, avoiding thousands of tiny canvases and repeated texture transfers. Terrain draws in an offline-compatible background worker.
- Starting terrain, build-menu meshes and initial unit poses are prepared before play; asset work yields to the UI. Nearby terrain is prefetched. Camera destinations switch only when their visible terrain is ready; flat-color placeholders have been removed. A distant uncached jump retains the previous view with a preparing-terrain notice. Large mixed armies may still encounter cold sprite-generation spikes; constant frame rate is not claimed.
- Construction names visible blockers, ignores exhausted/dead deposits, and clears decorative trees after payment/site validation. Building movement collision follows the drawn foundation with a small walking clearance. Tests exercise narrow passages at several placement offsets and solid interiors for all 175 structures.
- Paid building plans can be placed in explored fog using remembered terrain/objects. Plans grant no vision, block no movement, clear no trees and cannot be attacked. Workers check the actual site on arrival. Enemy occupation, new buildings, unsuitable terrain, vanished required deposits or an unreachable site cancel the plan with a single full refund. Unexplored footprint tiles stay prohibited. Manual cancellation of a plan also refunds fully; started foundations retain their 75% refund. Plans and reserved costs survive saves.
- Space centers the current selection. Ctrl/Shift-click adds units. Shift + number merges a saved group into the selection without saving; only Ctrl + number saves groups. Pause is on the Pause key and HUD button.
- Compact version-two saves load legacy version-one saves and retain exact terrain/knowledge. The 500-map browser save measured 880,671 characters. A four-player, 6,500-entity remembered-world stress case measured 1,583,456 characters (about 3.02 MiB UTF-16). This reduces large-map localStorage overflow; unusually large future states can still use file export.
- Design references were reviewed for varied materials, coastlines and readable height transitions: [StarCraft terrain/readability](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20726732/behind-the-scenes-of-starcraft-remastered), [Lost Temple geography](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/20930558/map-spotlight-the-lost-temple), and [Age of Empires II terrain presentation](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/visual-look-at-aoe2de/). No external artwork was imported.
