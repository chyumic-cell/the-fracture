## Bombers

All twelve civilization bomber equivalents now select the bomb projectile before faction-specific bullet, spell or biological weapon rules. The human unit is named Bomber. The review page says Air / Bombs, and the selection panel identifies these units as BOMBER.

Bombs release beneath the aircraft, fall to the ground, and explode after their flight time. They retain their original impact position rather than following moving targets. Blast damage respects combat matchups and armor, with full damage near the center and reduced outer damage; allies and aircraft are excluded. Existing two-salvo ammunition and automatic return/refuel/rearm behavior remain in place. Bomb release and impact use falling/explosion sounds.

Automated checks cover all twelve profiles, delayed damage, area damage, no friendly fire, a target escaping the blast, and returning with empty ammunition. Browser checks exercise the actual bomb renderer and labels, with screenshots in bomber-label-review.png and bomber-drop-review.png.

## AI repairs

- Counter selection now uses actual anti-air and combat multiplier fields, recent observations, and counters already fielded or queued.
- Detached raid and secondary squads are no longer routinely absorbed into reinforcements. Simultaneous raids use separate units and objectives.
- Secondary attacks require reachable, sufficiently vulnerable objectives and can continue toward remembered targets through fog. Visible disappearance or changed threats can cancel them.
- Reserve, counterattack, target-commitment, reconnaissance and precision-targeting flags now affect relevant decisions. Chokepoint analysis follows discovered routes and preserves fog limits. Paid mines are placed on the selected approach instead of being displaced by ordinary building placement searches.
- A pending trade recovery plan no longer blocks every combat purchase when the AI has surplus resources beyond its complete recovery reserve. Market placement explores more valid nearby positions.

The recorded island deadlock held approximately 7,505 ore with no ground army. Replaying that same saved state with the repair resumed production and ended in a Standard victory after 378 additional simulated seconds. See ai-island-recovery-replay.json. This is a recovery regression replay, **not a fresh ladder match or a win-rate sample**.

## Calibration evidence and limits

Before these changes, two side-swapped Apocalypse/Nightmare games and two Challenging/Standard games each split 1–1. These tiny screens exposed reversals; they cannot establish population win rates.

An experimental slower overall controller cadence caused worse top-tier performance and timeouts and was rejected. The original fast higher-tier cadence was restored. Its map-variety report belongs to that rejected snapshot and must not be presented as final-build win rates. A fast-cadence tactical snapshot also split the top pair 1–1 before the final recovery/bomber changes.

The scorecard now counts actual combat-unit production instead of counting initial workers and casualties as production. Reports fingerprint source and runner versions. Timeouts remain unresolved, and small observed percentages do not certify the target. All-map balance, a stable 5–10% band for every adjacent pair, and nonadjacent/FFA robustness remain unfinished.
