# Campaign illustration prompts and final assets

Tool: built-in image_gen, not CLI. Fourteen paintings completed in this release; thirty-six existing paintings retained. Reference: the existing Scene 8, shot 1 watercolor/colored-pencil image. Each painting is a separate scene, not a sprite atlas. Corrections removed surplus children in Scene 9 shot 4 and corrected Thread in Scene 10 shot 3.

Final directory: `assets/campaign/cinematics/`. New files: Scene 08 shots 02–05, Scene 09 shots 01–05, Scene 10 shots 01–05, named `scene_XX/image_YY.png`.
